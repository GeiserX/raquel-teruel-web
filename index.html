About me

Encantada de conocerte. Nice to meet you.

My name is Raquel and I’m from the Mediterranean coast of south-east Spain. I have taught Spanish to foreign students since 2014, and I have given more than 6,600 lessons to students from 60 countries.

I lived and worked in the UK, so I speak English fluently. For years I have specialised in Business Spanish, and in 2019 I created Go Native Spanish, the first free Business Spanish podcast.

My lessons are one to one and online, so wherever you live, we can learn together.

What a lesson feels like

My lessons are fun and relaxed, and you can make mistakes without worrying about them. Students tell me that in class they feel

en un lugar seguro

in a safe place.

You speak from the first lesson

I use a communicative approach. Spanish is for using, so we use it from day one.

Grammar that makes sense

I explain how the language works, so you understand it instead of memorising rules for their own sake.

Real material

News articles adapted to your level, videos, infographics and games, with plenty of culture and current events. That is how Spanish stays with you.

Why not just use an app?

A translator app can’t train your brain, open you up to another culture or help you win people over in their own language. Learning Spanish can.

Common questions

What level do I need?

Any level. I teach everyone from complete beginners (A1) to very advanced speakers (C2). In the trial lesson we see where you are.

I live in another time zone. Does that work?

Yes. I teach online from Spain, and when you book you will see the available times in your own time zone.

What do I need for the lessons?

A computer or tablet with a camera and a microphone, and a good internet connection.
