What I teach

Every lesson is one to one, online and 55 minutes long. We shape it around what you need Spanish for.

What a lesson feels like

en un lugar seguro

in a safe place

That's how my students say they feel in my lessons. Making mistakes is part of learning.

  • You speak from the first lesson

    I use a communicative approach, so you learn Spanish by using it, with you talking from the first lesson.

  • Grammar that makes sense

    I explain how and why Spanish works, so you understand the rules instead of memorising them for their own sake.

  • Real material

    News articles adapted to your level, videos, infographics and games, with culture and current events from the Spanish-speaking world.

  • Fun lessons

    Lessons are relaxed and enjoyable. You learn better when you're having a good time.

Why not just use a translator app?

An app can turn your words into Spanish. It can't train your brain, open you up to another culture, or help you win people over in their own language. Learning Spanish can.
