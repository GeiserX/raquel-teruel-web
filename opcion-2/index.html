About me

Encantada de conocerte. Nice to meet you. My name is Raquel and I come from the Mediterranean coast of south-east Spain.

I have taught Spanish to foreign students since 2014 and have given more than 6,600 lessons to people from 60 countries.

I lived and worked in the UK, where I was a Spanish language assistant at a secondary school through the British Council programme. I speak English fluently.

What a lesson feels like

I use a communicative approach.

  • You speak from the first lesson

    You learn Spanish by using it, and you speak from the very first lesson.

  • Grammar that makes sense

    I explain grammar so that you understand why it works. Never rules to memorise for their own sake.

  • Real material

    News articles adapted to your level, videos, infographics and games, with culture and current events.

  • A safe place to make mistakes

    Lessons are fun and relaxed. My students tell me they feel «en un lugar seguro»: in a safe place.

A translator app can’t train your brain, open you up to another culture or help you win people over in their own language. Learning Spanish can.

Questions

What level do I need?

None in particular. I teach every level, from complete beginners (A1) to advanced students (C2). In the free trial lesson we find out where you are.

I live in another time zone. Is that a problem?

Not at all. I teach online from Spain, and when you book, the times are shown in your own time zone.

What do I need for the lessons?

A computer or tablet with a camera and microphone, and a stable internet connection.
